# AGENTS.md — `sample-data/gavia/200906-mendums-pond/`

Field day of 1 June 2009 at Mendums Pond, Barrington NH, 18:00–21:16 UTC, in
support of Gavia AUV positioning tests. Two surface buoys, `RTK1` and `RTK2`,
each with a Trimble RTK GPS and an OceanServer-style tilt-compensated compass.

* `buoy/` — the four raw logs and a one-line `Makefile` (see its `AGENTS.md`).
* `mendums-pond-map-csv/` — empty; the processed CSV map products were not
  kept.

Nothing else. See `buoy/AGENTS.md` for the sentence formats.
